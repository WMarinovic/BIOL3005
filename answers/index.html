About these answers

This companion gives model answers to the self-check questions at the end of each chapter of Cognition: An open textbook, written for BIOL3005 Cognition at Curtin University. The answers are organised by chapter and numbered to match the questions in the textbook, and each question is reproduced above its answer.

Each answer sets out what a complete response covers, using the evidence, studies and terms that the chapter itself uses. They are not the only acceptable answers. A different answer that is accurate, uses the evidence well and reasons carefully can be just as good, and for the more open questions, such as those asking you to design a study or a revision plan, there are many good answers.

Try each question yourself before reading the answer. Retrieving an answer from memory, even an incomplete one, does far more for long-term retention than reading a correct answer (Chapter 5), so these answers work best as a way of checking and extending your own attempt. References to sections (for example, §4.4) are to the textbook, and the full references for every study mentioned are in the relevant chapter of the textbook.
