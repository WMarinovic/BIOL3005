Tutorial tools

Tutorial tools contain the experiment you run in the tutorial, your results, the class results and a quiz. Everything runs in your browser; your data stay on your computer unless you choose to share them.

Practice problems (Weeks 7 to 11) are exam-style scenarios to work through on your own after the tutorial, with hints and model answers. Try each question before you look.
