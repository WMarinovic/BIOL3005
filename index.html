Textbook

Twelve chapters, each with learning objectives, a summary and self-check questions. Start from the contents or from any week above.

Tutorial tools

The experiment you run in the tutorial, your results, the class results and a quiz. Everything runs in your browser; your data stay on your computer unless you choose to share them.

Practice problems and answers

Exam-style scenarios with hints and model answers, and model answers to every chapter’s self-check questions. Try each question before you look.
